# Bug hunt — 2026-07-06

Debugged the three reported screenshot issues (map scrub readout clipping, the
Diagnostics "Reconnect in Settings" recovery panel, and the DTC card labeling)
and ran a fan-out audit across the TypeScript dashboard, the Kotlin native
layer, the dashboard CSS, and the HTML partials. 55 verified defects were
found; the fixes in this change address them. Severity mix at triage: 1 high,
16 medium, 38 low.

Ranks are by severity/certainty. "Reported" marks the three user-circled
screenshot issues.

## Method

Findings were produced by a fan-out of per-area finder agents, each candidate
re-checked by an independent adversarial verifier, then deduped and ranked. A
handful of edge-case findings (NRC 0x78, TargetSocReached, sub-0.1 distance,
the Speed-chip wrap) were retained at low severity as plausible rather than
fully reproduced.
