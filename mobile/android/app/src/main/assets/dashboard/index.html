Adapter isn't connecting

Two attempts didn't connect. Walk through these one by one — most flaky sessions are fixed by step 1 or 2.

  1. Open the driver's door, press the brake pedal, or tap the start button without starting the engine. The OBD-II port goes to sleep after a few minutes of inactivity and refuses connections until something on the CAN bus wakes it.

  2. Pull the OBD adapter out of the port, count to ten, then plug it back in firmly. Watch for the status LED to come back on. 

       ┌───────────┐
   │ ◉  adapter│  ← pull out
   └─────┬─────┘
         │  wait 10s
   ┌─────┴─────┐
   │ ◉  adapter│  ← plug back in
   └───────────┘

    If the LED stays off after re-seating, the port may not be powered until the car is in accessory mode.

  3. Another app on this phone is also talking to the OBD adapter. Only one app can hold the Bluetooth serial channel at a time.

  4. One or more slow-tier PIDs haven't refreshed in a while. This usually means your adapter doesn't support Chevy Volt mode-22 PIDs (222429, 222414, 22434F).
